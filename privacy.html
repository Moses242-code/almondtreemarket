Privacy

Your details, kept simple.

We only ask for what we need to get your order ready and hand it to you.

What we keep

When you check out, we keep your name, phone number, pickup day, any note you add, and the items in your order. If you message us on WhatsApp or by email, we keep that conversation like any shop would.

Why

To get your order ready, to match you with your order when you come to collect (by QR code, or by name and phone), and to contact you if something is late or out of stock. We don't sell your details, and we don't send you promotions unless you ask us to.

Where it's kept

Orders are saved in our shop register (stored with our database provider, Supabase). When an order comes in, a short alert with your name, phone and items is sent to our own phones through notification services (ntfy and WhatsApp). Your cart and your QR codes are also kept in your own browser on your phone, so you can find them again under Your cart & orders.

No tracking

This website has no ads, no tracking and no analytics cookies.

Removing your details

Ask us any time and we'll delete your order details. Message us on WhatsApp (242) 809-5667 or email underthealmondtree242@gmail.com. To clear what's saved on your phone, empty your cart or clear this site's data in your browser.
