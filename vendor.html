      Questions

      Frequently asked

      How much is a slot?

      $25 per slot per day: a covered spot under the market roof, one table and one chair. No contract, no monthly fee.

      Which days can I sell?

      Any day, Monday through Sunday. Book one day or several. Special days are on the events calendar.

      Who can sell?

      Abaco growers and makers: produce, plants and fruit trees, straw work and crafts, candles and soaps, sauces and preserves, small food items and desserts.

      Do I have to come every day?

      No. Book one day, a few days, or ask to be a regular and keep the same slot on your days each week.

      How do I pay?

      Cash or bank transfer once we confirm. Your slot is held as soon as it's paid.

      What if it rains?

      The slots are under the market roof, so light rain is fine. If we have to cancel for weather, we'll message every booked vendor and your day moves to the next day we're open.
