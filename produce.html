Fruit from the yard, not the boat.

Mangoes, soursop, dillies, guineps, limes and pineapples, picked on Abaco and around the islands. Sold by the piece, never by the pound. Pick your fruit, tap Buy, get a QR code, and collect it any day we're open: Monday to Saturday (closed Sunday).

Pickup: your order is usually ready the same day, during business hours (Monday to Saturday)*, and we'll message you when it is. Then you have 24 hours to collect it with your QR code, or it goes back into stock.* If it will take more than 24 hours, we will let you know.

Cash only · pickup only: order online, pay cash when you collect at Almond Tree Market, Under the Almond Tree, Marsh Harbour. No deliveries. Prices are per piece (or per case, hand, bunch or bag where it says so), in Bahamian dollars. Fruit comes and goes with the seasons, so stock changes every week.
