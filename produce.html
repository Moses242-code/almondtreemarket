Fruit from the yard, not the boat.

Mangoes, soursop, dillies, guineps, limes and pineapples, picked on Abaco and around the islands. Sold by the piece, never by the pound. Pick your fruit, tap Reserve, get a QR code, and collect it any day we're open: Monday to Saturday (closed Sunday).

  1. 01Choose your fruit and how many, tap Reserve
  2. 02Pick a pickup day and get your QR code
  3. 03We message you when it's ready. Come in, scan, pay and take it home

Pickup: we need up to 24 hours* to get your order ready, and we'll message you when it is. Then you have 24 hours to collect it with your QR code, or it goes back into stock.* Sometimes it can take more than 24 hours, depending on where your item is. We will let you know.

Prices are per piece (or per hand, bunch or bag where it says so), in Bahamian dollars. Fruit comes and goes with the seasons, so stock changes every week.
